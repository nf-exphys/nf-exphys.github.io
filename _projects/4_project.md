I work as part of the [Sweetpea](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06548828) study led by Dr. Allison Sylvetsky. I am responsible for teaching research assistants how to perform laboratory measures, providing ongoing supervision of research assistants, and assisting with processing and storing biospecimens in a timely and organized manner. I also wrote SOPs and designed a Google form system to automatically notify the laboratory team of upcoming research visits and prepare the appropriate spreadsheets and forms automatically. This has saved time and improved communication across the team.
